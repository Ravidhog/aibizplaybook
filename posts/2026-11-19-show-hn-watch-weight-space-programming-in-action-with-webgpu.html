Show HN: Watch weight-space programming in action with WebGPU

Published: 2026-11-19T15:32:47+00:00

I wanted to build a full weight-space programming loop entirely in the browser with WebGPU: let an LLM inspect Qwen3.5-0.8B, edit its weights, evaluate, and repeat.

the full loop needed too much VRAM. so I turned it into an interactive walkthrough of a concrete example: editing 0.016% of a model's weights to reduce reasoning loops, no gradient descent.

Based on exp8 of “Sparks of Weight-Space Programming.”: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50341174
